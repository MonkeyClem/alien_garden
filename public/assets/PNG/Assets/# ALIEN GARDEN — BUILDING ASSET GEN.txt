# ALIEN GARDEN — BUILDING ASSET GENERATION V1

Create a new isometric 2.5D pixel-art building for the video game **Alien Garden**.

**CRITICAL: Use the supplied final OutpostHQ asset as the authoritative geometric reference.**

The new building must use exactly the same ground-plane perspective, footprint edge slopes, camera orientation and ground-contact geometry as the reference building.

## 1. Mandatory ground geometry

- Game grid: **3:1 isometric diamond projection**.
- Individual ground tile dimensions: **60 px wide × 20 px high**.
- Ground-plane directions: **+18.435° and -18.435° relative to horizontal**.
- Ground-plane edge slopes: **+1/3 and -1/3**.
- Vertical structural edges must remain vertical.
- All foundation edges must remain parallel to the corresponding grid directions.
- The building must sit naturally on the ground, without appearing to float or rotate relative to the grid.
- Every ground-contact corner must align with the intended grid footprint.

**Do not use conventional 2:1 isometric perspective or 30-degree ground-plane edges.**

## 2. Reference asset

Use the attached approved OutpostHQ as a strict reference for:

- Ground-contact perspective.
- Foundation proportions.
- Direction and inclination of both visible façades.
- Relationship between building width, depth and vertical height.
- Pixel-art rendering, outlines, shading and architectural detailing.

**Preserve its geometric projection, not necessarily its architectural design.**

The new building may have a different silhouette, roof, function and decorative elements, but its foundation must follow the same projection.

## 3. Art direction

Alien Garden is a retro-futuristic science-fiction RPG inspired by late-1990s and early-2000s JRPG pixel art.

Human outpost architecture uses:

- Off-white and cream metallic panels.
- Dark graphite structural components.
- Orange industrial accents.
- Cyan illuminated windows, sensors and equipment.
- Detailed but readable pixel-art shading.
- Consistent outlines and pixel density.

The building should visually belong to the same scientific outpost as the approved OutpostHQ, Seed Bank and BioScanner.

## 5. Output requirements

- One standalone building asset.
- Transparent PNG background with real alpha transparency.
- No terrain, vegetation, painted ground texture or grid lines.
- No perspective-distorting decorative base.
- No oversized stairs or platforms extending beyond the intended footprint.
- Keep the complete building visible.
- Preserve clean, readable pixel-art details.

**PRIORITY ORDER:**

1. Exact ground-plane geometry.
2. Consistent building proportions.
3. Art-direction consistency.
4. Architectural details.

If a decorative element conflicts with the required ground geometry, simplify or remove that element rather than changing the projection.
